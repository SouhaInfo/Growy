# Targeted Individuals

### What is the age range of your target audience?
18 and above, responsible for child care and child development.

### Will your site appeal to more women or men? What is the mix?
It will appeal to both men and women, as child care is a shared responsibility.

### Which country do your visitors live in?
Algeria.

### Do they live in urban or rural areas?
Anyone with internet access who has an interest in child care, whether from urban or rural areas, can explore the website.

### What is the average income of visitors?
There are both free and paid services available, so visitors can choose according to their needs.

### What level of education do they have?
Basic reading and writing skills are necessary to use the website. Additional skills depend on the user's role. For example, a math teacher should have knowledge of mathematics if they are seeking a job.

### What is their occupation?
It varies depending on the visitor. For parents, occupation doesn’t matter. For users offering services or making announcements, their occupation should be related to the services they provide.

### How many hours do they work per week?
This doesn’t matter.

### How often do they use the web?
Daily, as they are regular users.

### What kind of device do they use to access the web?
Any device with internet access (mobile phones, tablets, computers).

---

# Targeted Companies

### What is the size of the company or relevant department?
The site focuses mainly on regular users, but it is flexible enough to attract organizations, such as language schools, private schools, kindergartens, and clubs offering services for children.

### What is the position of people in the company who visit your site?
Managers, administrators, and marketing personnel.

### Will visitors be using the site for themselves or for someone else?
Both. For example, schools may use it to share announcements about their services, while individuals can use it for personal needs.

### How large is the budget they control?
This depends on the size of the company.

---

# Why People Visit the Website

### Key Motivations

#### Are they looking for general entertainment or to achieve a specific goal?
The website is goal-driven, focused on child care, not for entertainment.

#### If there is a specific goal, is it personal or professional?
The goals are both personal and professional. Personally, users can share experiences and learn about child development, health, and cognitive growth. Professionally, users can make announcements, offer jobs, take or share courses, all of which contribute to building a strong CV.

#### Do they see spending time on this activity as essential or a luxury?
It’s essential, as it helps them with child care, finding babysitters, teachers, schools, clubs, and courses, as well as obtaining opinions on their contributions.

---

# Specific Goals

### Do they want general information or something specific?
Parents will likely visit for specific information related to child care and finding services for their child’s growth and development. Other users, like service providers, can tailor their services based on parents' needs.

### Are they already familiar with the service or product you offer, or do they need an introduction?
They may be familiar with similar services, but not specifically in the context of child care.

### Are they looking for time-sensitive information (e.g., job offers or updates)?
Yes, particularly for job offers or services provided by others.

### Do they want to discover information to help them decide whether to purchase a product/service?
Yes. For example, they can review course details to decide whether to enroll, or compare teachers and babysitters to find the best fit for their needs.

### Do they need to contact you? If so, how?
Yes, users can contact us for feedback or issues related to the website. Additionally, the contribution section allows users to submit their content, which requires our approval.
